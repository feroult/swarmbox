● The bridge had no name until the winter Marta started crossing it.

  Locals just called it "the span," a rusted arc of iron over a river so wide that fog swallowed the far bank each
  morning. Nobody remembered who built it. Nobody much cared, until the ferry stopped running and the span became the
  only way across.

  Marta was nine, new to the village, and sure the bridge was alive. She said so to her grandfather on the first day of
  school. "It hums," she told him.

  "That's wind in the cables."

  "It's humming."

  He let it go. He was seventy-one and had stopped arguing with certainty sometime around his wife's funeral.
