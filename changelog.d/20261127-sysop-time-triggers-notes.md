- Added: the sysop keeps time and watches the box: ask it to check something every 10 minutes, at 18:00, or when a tab becomes blocked or prints a text, and it creates a schedule or a trigger that the host runs as a goal (pause, resume and cancel them in the Events tab of the Sysop panel, or with `swarmbox-sysop automation`). Every automation ends by itself, and one started by an automation cannot create another without your approval.
- Added: the sysop can ask you a question with buttons and wait for the answer, send you a notification (a toast in every open Web Studio and, once allowed in Settings → Sysop, the browser's own), keep notes across conversations, and read a log or a file of the box with its secrets removed instead of typing `cat` into a tab.
- Changed: a message you type or speak while the sysop works on a goal now reaches that goal at once ("no, the other tab") instead of waiting for it to end.
